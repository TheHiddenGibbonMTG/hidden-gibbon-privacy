Gibbon's Games with Friends

Privacy Policy

Developer: Hidden Gibbon

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

1. Overview

Gibbon's Games with Friends is an unofficial Magic: The Gathering life and statistics tracking application. Hidden Gibbon respects user privacy and aims to minimize the collection of personal information.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The application stores gameplay information locally on your device. This may include player names, deck and commander names, life totals, game results, statistics, counters, and application preferences.

Hidden Gibbon does not operate a server that collects or stores these gameplay records.

3. Internet Access and Scryfall

The application uses the Scryfall API to retrieve publicly available Magic: The Gathering card information and images.

When connecting to Scryfall, your device sends network requests directly to Scryfall's services. Technical information, including your IP address, may be processed by Scryfall as part of normal internet communication.

Scryfall is an independent third-party service. For more information, visit Scryfall's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Collection and Sharing

Hidden Gibbon does not intentionally collect, sell, or transmit locally stored gameplay statistics to its own servers or advertisers.

The application does not include advertising or analytics SDKs in the reviewed release.

Users may voluntarily export and share their own gameplay data using the application's backup and export functions.

5. Backups and Data Export

The application supports manual data export and import. Exported files are saved to locations selected by the user through the device's file selection interface.

Users are responsible for managing, protecting, and deleting their exported backup files.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information stored locally remains on the device until removed through available application controls or by clearing the application's storage.

Uninstalling the application generally removes its local application data, although exported files and copies retained by device backup services may remain separately.

Hidden Gibbon cannot remotely delete locally stored gameplay information because it does not maintain a central database of those records.

7. External Links

The application may provide links to external websites and services, including Scryfall, YouTube, Archidekt, and social media platforms.

These services operate independently and have their own privacy policies and data practices.

8. Children's Privacy

The application is not specifically directed toward children and does not intentionally request personal information from children.

9. Security

The application is designed to minimize unnecessary data collection and store gameplay records locally. However, no application or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when application features, data practices, or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this webpage.
